FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which phone models are compatible? +

Our cases are available for iPhone 13, 14, 15, 15 Pro, 15 Pro Max, 16, 16 Pro, 16 Pro Max, 17, 17 Pro and 17 Pro Max, and for Samsung Galaxy S24, S24 Ultra, S25, S25 Ultra, S26, S26 Ultra, A36 5G and A56 5G. You choose the model on the product page: each case is made for the selected model, so please check it before placing your order.

How are your cases made? +

We start from clear cases that we print ourselves, one by one, in France. The ink is applied directly onto the case, with no film or sticker in between.

What are the delivery times? +

In mainland France, your parcel reaches your Mondial Relay pickup point 4 to 7 business days after your order (home delivery is coming soon). Allow 5 to 8 days to a pickup point in Belgium and Luxembourg, 5 to 9 days elsewhere in the European Union, and up to 14 days to the United States and Canada. You receive a tracking number as soon as your order ships.

Can I return an item? +

Yes, you have 14 days after receipt to change your mind. The product must be returned in its original condition. See our dedicated page for the procedure.

I have an idea but don't know how to make it +

No problem, we'll take care of it! In the studio, open the Help tab: describe your idea, add your photos or inspiration, and our workshop creates your design. You receive it by email within 48 to 72 hours, free and with no obligation.

Can a custom case be returned? +

A case created in the studio is made from your own design, just for you: it cannot be resold, so it is not covered by the right of withdrawal. Please check your preview carefully before ordering. A manufacturing defect is of course always covered.
