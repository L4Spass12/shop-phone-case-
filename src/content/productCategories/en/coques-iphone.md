## Your model first

Each case is moulded for one specific model: check the exact name of your iPhone (Settings, then About) before ordering. An iPhone 16 Pro and a 16 Pro Max differ in size and camera layout.

## Flat or raised

Flat printing gives a sharp, glossy result, ideal for graphic patterns. Raised printing brings out detailed illustrations: clouds, flowers, textures. Both are printed on a clear case that lets your phone's colour show around the pattern.

## Your own design

With the design studio, add your photos, your text and our graphic elements. The file is printed in high definition on your case.
