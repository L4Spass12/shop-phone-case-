## A case made for your Galaxy

Each case is moulded for one specific model: buttons, USB-C port and camera block line up exactly. The design is then printed one at a time, to order, on a clear case.

## Flat or raised

Flat printing gives a smooth finish and vivid colours, ideal for graphic designs and photos. Raised printing lays the ink in several passes: you can feel the design under your finger and detailed illustrations gain depth.

## Your own design

With the [design studio](/en/personnaliser/), create a [custom phone case](/en/coque-personnalisee/) with your photos, a name or a collage. The preview adapts to your Galaxy model.
